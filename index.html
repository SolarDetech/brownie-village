Where shall we go?
A WORLD, NOT A MENU

Find your way.

Explore

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click the minimap to travel. The home button centres your village.

Read the agents

Each lead shows a bubble over their head: a spinning gear when working, dots when idle, an amber ? when your approval is needed, a red ! on error, and floating Z's when offline. The district's flag and equipment show the same state.

Visit

Click a district or its label. Agents open drawers. The HQ and hospital open modals. Head to the western woods for the Colosseum.

Get things done

North: Command Grounds, the Grand Library and Healing Gardens. South: the lumberyard, mine and farm, worked by Köle crews. The post office & court (GGI in GKTC, DGI in Daghan) is your Inbox.

Your avatars

GKTC’s Megazord waits at GKTC’s HQ and Daghan’s Gojo at Daghan’s. Drag either one into a district: its lead walks over, walks alongside and talks.

Keyboard

/ opens the atlas. On the map, arrows pan, + / − zoom, and 0 fits the world. Escape closes a drawer.

Quiet moments

The pause button stops animation. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

All activity is sample data. Notes, approvals and state changes affect this local prototype only.
